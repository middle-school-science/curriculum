## Images, other works and marks

The PDFs explain that their images are public domain, openly licensed or used by agreement, and direct readers to individual image attributions for restrictions. The collection-wide license does not override any specifically stated exception or third-party term. Keep these notices with the originals. Videos, externally hosted documents and simulations linked inside a PDF have not been copied merely because the link appears there.

The OpenSciEd name and logo are excluded from the Creative Commons license. This independent collection does not use them as its own branding, claim endorsement, or award certification to modified materials. Original notices and marks remain within unchanged documents for identification and attribution.
